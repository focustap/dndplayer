# The Hollow — Greymere preservation reference

DM ONLY. Transcribed from the existing live Warden note, 2026-09-16. This records existing canon; it does not revise dialogue, encounters or player state.

GREYMERE CATALYST — HARLAN VOSS / THE WARDEN

Core idea
Voss was a real Greymere protector years before the current disappearances. His personal sorcery was modest: by physically drawing a closed circle around a campsite, he could briefly discourage or block ordinary beasts and physical threats from crossing inward. Hunters called him the Warden. He disappeared after investigating the strange dead patch north of town and tried to contain it with his own technique. The Hollow corrupted him and amplified that same ability into Closed Circle, the existing boss mechanic that seals off parts of the room. His original instinct—keep danger out—has rotted into keep people in.

DO NOT CHANGE HOBB. His “Whole world’s got walls...” line remains unrelated campaign-scale foreshadowing about the world itself being a Hollow. He is supposed to sound insane.

Scene clues
GREYMERE: One guard remembers Voss as an old protector who drew circles around camps. Another says Edrin recently found a fresh-looking Warden mark near his traps. Neither guard thinks Voss could actually be alive.
CROOKED STAG: Jimmy explicitly distinguishes the old circular Warden marks from VII, preserving VII as a separate mystery. The Suspicious Man warns that a Catalyst can be something the Hollow twisted rather than something newly created.
NORTH WOODS: Keep VII and the wolves as-is. If the party searches beyond the marked tree, optional DC 13 Investigation: find a faded chalk/charcoal circle around an old firepit. It is harmless and years old—the kind of ward Voss used before corruption.
EDRIN’S CAMP: Keep all existing checks. When Edrin’s Knife is found, DC 12 Investigation notices a small hand-scratched circle on the inside of the sheath: Edrin copied the mark he found near his traps. A successful Survival check can also find a partial ring scored into the dirt at the edge of camp, broken outward where something was dragged away.
HOLLOW APPROACH: No new roll required. Describe one old ring of stones beside the path. The stones on the outside are untouched; the stones on the inside have been shoved outward.
DUNGEON ROOM 1: Keep zombies. Optional detail: one corpse has a faint white circular scar around one wrist, as though a boundary had once been drawn directly around it. Do not explain it.
PUZZLE ROOM: Keep the Tongue riddle unchanged.
CATALYST ROOM: Keep the current Wight stats and circular cut-off mechanic. Rename the instance The Warden. The circles are Closed Circle, Voss’s amplified personal technique. The prisoners confirm that he repeatedly called captivity “inside” and the world beyond his boundaries “outside.”

Boss portrayal
Voss is not cackling or sadistic. He is calm, possessive, and sincerely convinced containment is protection. Good lines: “Stay inside the line.” “I can keep you safe.” “Stop leaving.” At low HP: “Why do you keep choosing the outside?”

What players can conclude
They can learn that Voss had a strange boundary technique BEFORE he became the Catalyst, and that the Hollow amplified/corrupted something already present in him. Do not state that every Catalyst works this way. This is an early example, not the full cosmology.
