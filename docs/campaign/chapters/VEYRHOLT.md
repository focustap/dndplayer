# The Hollow — Chapter 2: Veyrholt

## Chapter contract

- **Entry state:** Pipp Karew (Jess, Druid), Gerbo Grumble (Aiden, Gnome Paladin), Theldren Eldercrown (Cody, Wizard), and Echo Frost (Zach, Rogue) have defeated the Greymere Catalyst. They are Level 2, possess a Catalyst Fragment, and have rescued Pipp's father, Dog, and the captured villagers.
- **Road milestone:** The Messenger's summons and the Bellpost Road adventure take the party from Level 2 to Level 3 before Veyrholt.
- **Veyrholt level:** Encounters assume four Level 3 characters. Award Level 4 after the Veyrholt Catalyst is destroyed.
- **Chapter identity:** Veyrholt's Hollow records physical acts, sounds, and spellwork, then performs a dangerous **Second Motion** several heartbeats later. The castle and town bells once kept those repetitions predictable.
- **Player-facing revelation:** At least one Catalyst was deliberately built by people who already knew how to shape a Hollow. A Catalyst can be both a sustaining heart and an attempted restraint; Hollows and their Catalysts are not all naturally occurring monster lairs.
- **DM-only restraint:** Do not reveal that Elyndra is itself a Hollow, that the party was placed in it, or what the Threshold truly is. Nothing in this chapter proves why the Greymere and Veyrholt events share a timestamp.
- **Following hook:** When the castle falls silent, a distant answering bell and an altered ward ledger point toward **Lornwatch Abbey** on the eastern Glass Road.

## Chapter at a glance

1. **Greymere aftermath:** The Hearthstone returns the party to the Crooked Stag. The Messenger arrives with Veyrholt's urgent sealed appeal and a private request from Pipp's father.
2. **Bellpost Road:** A caravan is trapped in a repeating stretch of road after an old bellpost was stripped. The party rescues the travelers, fights opportunistic marauders and hostile road-repetitions, and reaches Level 3.
3. **Veyrholt:** A prosperous castle-town has lived beside a predictable Hollow for generations. Recent double tolls, repeated movements, and livestock thefts threaten the arrangement.
4. **The missing tithe:** What looks like ordinary rustling is an ancient collection route repeating itself. The stolen bell-bearing animals and their bronze bells are being taken toward the castle.
5. **The ring road:** The investigation reveals safe approaches, the rule of Second Motion, and three possible ways into Castle Veyr.
6. **Castle Veyr:** The party uses deliberate repetitions to cross the gate, free the Echo Pens, navigate the Hall of Measures, recover the ward history, and descend through the Bellfoundry.
7. **The Bell Regent:** A caretaker fused to the anchor mechanism weaponizes recorded actions. The party can turn its own repetitions against the anchor bells.
8. **Aftermath:** The Veyrholt Hollow collapses, the tithed creatures return, every ward bell cracks, and the party learns that the castle's Catalyst was constructed. A signal from Lornwatch supplies the next destination.

## Truth layers

### Safe for players to learn

- Veyrholt's castle has been uncanny for longer than anyone alive can remember.
- The town survived by maintaining bellposts, marked roads, and strict crossing customs.
- The recent instability began at the same recorded minute as the Greymere Catalyst's destruction.
- The missing livestock were following a replay of an old tithe route.
- Castle Veyr was built around an existing anomaly; its central mechanism became the local Catalyst.
- Old ward-builders understood enough to make several sites “answer” one another, but the surviving records do not explain why.
- The mark `VII` appears in an old engineering context, but its meaning remains unresolved.

### DM-only truths for this chapter

- The **Second Motion** is the Veyrholt Hollow's way of preserving an action without preserving its intent. It can replay kindness, violence, labor, or spellwork with equal indifference.
- The Bell Regent was once a willing mortal custodian named **Ser Caldris Veyr**. The anchor preserved his last duty until it replaced his identity.
- The distributed bronze bells around Veyrholt were part of the containment lattice. Generations treated them as folk custom and gradually forgot their purpose.
- Bellwright Nessa Calder's sale and recasting of old ward bronze weakened the lattice but did not awaken it. She is culpable for fragility, not for the initiating event.
- The first uncontrolled double toll was recorded within the same minute that the Greymere Catalyst died. This is correlation, not a confirmed cause.
- The Catalyst Fragment makes the Bell Regent identify the party as a “received response.” This implies communication or resonance between sites, but not its source or scope.
- A damaged diagram contains an unlabeled doorway drawn differently in three copies. It is permitted Threshold foreshadowing only: never name or explain the Threshold to the players here.

If the party uses the Hearthstone, they arrive in the Crooked Stag amid survivors, exhausted guards, and villagers trying to account for the rescued. ### Why every character goes

It reacts to the Catalyst Fragment.

- **Echo Frost:** Dog refuses to let the Messenger's satchel out of sight and reacts several heartbeats before the fragment pulses. The missing caravan gives Echo a concrete trail and lets Dog's altered instincts matter without forcing Echo into a faction.

- Pipp's father remembers a Varakai warning: “A quiet bell is not the same as an empty tower.” He does not know its origin.
- A rescued villager says the Greymere mage-Catalyst sometimes paused as if listening to a sound beyond the room.
- Dog occasionally watches a person finish an action, then watches the empty place where the action began.
- Jimmy Frog offers one last ordinary meal and refuses payment from the rescuers.

## Act I: Bellpost Road

### Scene R1 — The repeated mile

**Arrival:** Rain-dark road, an overturned blue caravan, and the same lightning-split elm visible both ahead and behind. A rider's shout repeats from the empty air after six heartbeats.

**Immediately visible**

- Wagon tracks lead forward, then overlay themselves in the opposite direction.
- The roadside bellpost has been stripped of its bronze handbell.
- Three travelers shelter beneath the wagon; one injured courier is stranded thirty feet away but appears to snap back whenever the second toll sounds.
- Chalk arrows on the road recur in sets of seven.

**Baseline clue:** Anyone who watches for one full cycle sees that movements made after the first distant toll are replayed at the second. No roll is needed.

**Checks**

- DC 11 Survival: distinguish the travelers' real tracks from repeated impressions; advantage for Echo if Dog helps.
- DC 12 Arcana: the replay is not an illusion; it applies force and repeats magical effects at reduced strength.
- DC 12 Nature: local birds avoid landing between the bellpost and the elm, marking the affected boundary.
- DC 13 Investigation: find the missing handbell's drag marks leading to a culvert.
- DC 14 Religion: Gerbo recognizes the bellpost's seven-knot cord as a ward custom, not a roadside shrine.

**Rescue mechanic: the count of six**

The party can reach each traveler by moving immediately after a toll, then stopping off the visible track before the replay. A failed check or poor plan costs a Hit Die-sized complication (1d6 force damage or separation), never a dead end. Cutting the wagon loose, using rope from outside the loop, or deliberately sending an echo to drag it all work.

**Exits:** The culvert leads to R2. Restoring the handbell ends the loop after the marauders are dealt with. Walking away leaves the caravan trapped and delays Veyrholt's food delivery; the Messenger or a survivor points out the culvert so progress remains possible.

### Scene R2 — Culvert of the Toll-Takers

The **Greyhook crew**, ordinary road thieves, removed the bell believing it was valuable old bronze. They did not create the loop and are now trapped on its edge with things made from their repeated violence.

#### Combat: Toll-Takers and Road Refrains

**Trigger:** The party reaches the stolen bell or the Greyhooks try to bargain with the trapped travelers as leverage.

**Enemies**

- 1 Greyhook scout: use Scout with 18 HP and no Multiattack.
- 2 bandits.
- 2 Road Refrains: AC 12, HP 9, speed 30; slam +4, 1d6+2 force. A Refrain repeats the last weapon attack made within 10 feet of it against the attacker's former space on the following initiative count.

**Terrain / mechanic**

- Shallow rushing water is difficult terrain.
- Three stone arches provide half cover.
- On initiative 10, the last creature to cross the central channel leaves a forceful replay that travels the same line; DC 12 Dexterity save or 1d6 force damage and pushed 5 feet.
- A character adjacent to the handbell can use an action to ring it, suppressing all Refrains until initiative 10 next round.

**Tactics**

- The scout fires from the high drain shelf and surrenders if both bandits fall.
- Bandits shove targets into the replay line rather than fight to the death.
- Refrains repeat aggression without choosing sides; clever positioning can make them hit bandits.
- The thieves flee if offered a safe route out of the loop.

**Scaling**

- Easier: remove one Road Refrain and give the injured courier one Help action.
- Harder: restore the scout to 24 HP or add one bandit, not both.

**Reward / clue**

- 18 gp, trade goods worth 12 gp, and the stolen handbell.
- The bell bears the same seven-notch foundry mark seen on the Messenger's seal and Pipp's father's tuning key.
- One bandit heard the empty road say, “Tithe accepted,” after stealing it.
- Restoring the bell ends the repeated mile and earns Veyrholt's 40 gp rescue payment.

### Road milestone

When the caravan is free and the group completes a long rest, advance all four characters to **Level 3**. This milestone applies even if the Greyhooks were negotiated with or escaped; solving the loop and rescuing the travelers is the accomplishment.

## Veyrholt overview

### Town identity

Veyrholt is a practical hill-town of slate roofs, pale timber, copper gutters, and orchards built around the massive silhouette of **Castle Veyr**. Roads divide and reunite around old stone markers. Farms run almost to the castle's ring road. For generations, locals have treated the Hollow as dangerous infrastructure rather than an expanding apocalypse.

Every public building has a bell. Locals pause briefly at the first toll and move after the second. Most cannot explain why beyond, “That's how you keep your shadow behind you.” Until six nights ago, the castle tolls were perfectly predictable.

### What changed

- Bells sometimes ring a second time without being struck.
- A completed action repeats several heartbeats later: a dropped cup falls again, a gate recloses, an axe stroke appears in empty air.
- Livestock vanish from latched fields, always animals wearing old bronze bells.
- The ring road's reliable safe intervals now shift.
- Castle windows show meals, patrols, and ceremonies from different centuries.
- The first uncontrolled double toll occurred at the same minute as the Greymere Catalyst's destruction.

### Town locations

#### V1 — South Gate and Sevenfold Road

- **Visible:** Seven parallel wear-lines converge into one road beneath a squat gatehouse. Guards chalk the safe line each morning.
- **Interactables:** Toll ledger, cracked warning bell, caravan board, old route stone.
- **Checks:** DC 11 Perception notices one chalk line is six inches out of date; DC 13 History recognizes the gate predates most town buildings; DC 14 Arcana detects the road is repeatedly “remembered” into its old position.
- **NPCs:** Captain Bryn Halvek and two gate guards.
- **Purpose:** Establish normal life adapted to the Hollow and give access to the missing-tithe case.
- **Exits:** Brass Lamb, market, Reeve's Hall, west farms, castle ring road.

#### V2 — The Brass Lamb

- **Visible:** Warm inn with dozens of retired livestock bells hung from rafters. They sway but do not ring during a Second Motion.
- **Interactables:** Rumor board, repaired caravan chest, hearth map scratched into a table, one silent bell with `VII` inside its lip.
- **Checks:** DC 12 Insight separates panic from useful testimony; DC 13 Investigation finds all retired bells were cast before the current bellwright's tenure.
- **NPCs:** Innkeeper Maura Fen, shepherd Tamsin Reed, traveling peddlers.
- **Purpose:** Safe hub, rumors, lodging, and the first apparently mundane livestock-theft pitch.
- **Shop:** Common adventuring supplies at normal prices, two healing potions at 55 gp each, silvered sling bullets (10) for 25 gp, and one **Bellpost Charm** for 35 gp (once, gain advantage on a save against fear, charm, or forced movement; charm cracks after use).

#### V3 — Reeve's Hall and Archive

- **Visible:** Civic chamber sharing a wall with a cramped archive. Three clocks disagree by exactly six seconds.
- **Interactables:** Incident map, sealed clock ledger, tax rolls, copies of castle crossing rules.
- **Checks:** DC 12 Persuasion obtains open records; DC 14 Investigation finds the old tithe route beneath modern property lines; DC 15 Insight reveals Reeve Morrow is concealing the activation timestamp.
- **NPCs:** Reeve Elian Morrow, Archivist Sister Avra Seln.
- **Purpose:** Main investigation board, town authority, timing correlation, and old route map.

#### V4 — Calder Bellworks

- **Visible:** Foundry built over a spring, crowded with molds and green-black bronze filings. Finished bells are deliberately muted with cloth.
- **Interactables:** Alloy samples, commission ledger, cracked ward bell, resonance table.
- **Checks:** DC 11 Smith's tools or Investigation matches road and livestock bells to castle bronze; DC 13 Arcana learns their tones damp Second Motions; DC 15 Investigation finds sales of recast ward bronze hidden as scrap.
- **NPCs:** Nessa Calder and apprentice Pell.
- **Purpose:** Explain the bell network, expose Nessa's secret, and provide tools for the castle.
- **Reward:** If treated as an ally, Nessa makes four wax-and-bronze ear seals (advantage on the first save against a sound-based castle effect) and tunes Pipp's father's key to open the Tithe Gate.

#### V5 — Sunward Farms

- **Visible:** Productive orchards and sheep fields pressed close to the castle road. Several gates are tied shut over intact latches.
- **Interactables:** Empty bell collars, drag-free hoofprints, old tithe stones used as fence posts, a lamb that returns to its former position every second toll.
- **Checks:** DC 10 Animal Handling calms the lamb; DC 12 Survival tracks animals to a tithe stone where prints stop; DC 13 Nature notices only bell-bearing livestock are taken; DC 14 Investigation finds blue-green bronze dust in a latched gate.
- **NPCs:** Tamsin Reed, neighboring farmers, Dog if accompanying Echo.
- **Purpose:** Establish the theft pattern and provide a trail to the Old Tithe Barn.

#### V6 — Chapel of the Last Chime

- **Visible:** Small roadside chapel whose bell rope ends several feet above the floor. Parishioners use poles rather than stand beneath it.
- **Interactables:** Seven-verse maintenance litany, founder relief, censored verse, undercroft door.
- **Checks:** DC 12 Religion interprets the litany as operating instructions; DC 13 Perception finds the plastered-over seventh verse; DC 15 History dates its doorway to the same period as Castle Veyr.
- **NPCs:** Sister Avra when not at the archive.
- **Purpose:** Alternate castle entrance, lore delivery, and Gerbo roleplay about duty becoming empty repetition.

#### V7 — Old Tithe Barn

- **Visible:** Disused stone barn on the former castle collection road. Fresh feed is untouched, but the dust shows hooves appearing in place rather than entering.
- **Interactables:** Smuggler cache, tax tags, half-present livestock, culvert map.
- **Purpose:** Reveal the “rustling” as an ancient replay and lead directly to Castle Veyr.

#### V8 — Castle Veyr and ring road

- **Visible:** A broad, inhabited-looking castle inside a faintly silver boundary. Every few moments a different historical banner hangs from the same tower.
- **Interactables:** Bellposts, patrol markers, three entrances, returning footprints.
- **Purpose:** Chapter dungeon and Veyrholt Hollow.

## Investigation: the missing tithe

The players need **two of the following four conclusions** to locate the Old Tithe Barn. No single failed roll blocks progress.

1. **Farm pattern:** Sunward Farms shows only inherited bell-bearing livestock disappear. The oldest affected properties sit on the former tithe road.
2. **Bronze pattern:** Calder Bellworks identifies the collars as castle bronze and admits the alloy suppresses Second Motions.
3. **Record pattern:** The Reeve's tax map overlays the incidents with the abandoned tithe route.
4. **Witness pattern:** Kest or Tamsin saw the silent procession turn toward the Old Tithe Barn.

If the party stalls, a midday double toll makes an untouched sheep begin walking through fences toward the barn. Following it supplies the route but begins the barn encounter with the enemies alert.

### Roleplay pressure

- Bryn wants Kest arrested before evidence is complete.
- Nessa wants the old bells seized as civic property.
- Tamsin refuses to surrender the few bells left on her flock.
- Mara warns Pipp that factions may treat the Fragment as evidence, weapon, or key.
- Reeve Morrow offers a quick payment for a quiet resolution and a larger public reward for proof.

The party can expose Nessa, protect her, demand restitution, or ask her to repair the lattice. This choice changes town support but not access to the castle.

## Approaching Castle Veyr

### Hollow boundary rule

Inside the ring road, a distinct toll occurs every other round or roughly every twelve seconds outside combat:

1. **First toll — Record:** Significant movement, attacks, loud sounds, and spells leave a pale afterimage.
2. **Second toll — Replay:** The afterimage repeats the motion without intent. Replayed attacks can hit any creature in their original line. Replayed movement may push through a space. Replayed utility can be helpful.

Tell players when an action leaves a visible record. Do not make the mechanic a surprise damage tax after its first demonstration. Dog growls at a recorded space one beat before it replays, giving Echo a reliable warning.

### Three entrances

- **Gatehouse route:** Bryn's chalk path and Nessa's tuned key. Safest navigation, but it passes the Courtyard Sentinels.
- **Chapel undercroft:** Avra's seventh verse opens a reliquary stair into the Hall of Measures. Skips the gate fight but adds the Choir Stair hazard.
- **Smuggler culvert:** Kest's map reaches the Bellfoundry. It allows a surprise attack there but leaves the Echo Pens unresolved until the party backtracks or frees them from the control room.

If the party lacks all helpers, the visible tithe procession opens the main gate at dusk. They can follow it automatically.

## Castle dungeon

### C1 — The Tithe Gate

**Arrival:** An iron gate stands open and closed at once, alternating at each toll. Bronze animal bells crawl across the stones toward sockets in the wall.

**Interactables / discoverables**

- Two pressure plates six seconds of travel apart.
- A relief of workers standing aside while their afterimages continue laboring.
- **Discoverable: Gatewright's Lesson** — “Make the first motion. Leave the second to the Keep.” Purpose: states the core puzzle rule.

**Puzzle:** One character activates the first plate, steps away, and lets their replay hold it while another activates the second. Mage Hand, a familiar, weights, or breaking the gate also works. A failed timing attempt triggers 1d6 force damage and resets; it never jams permanently.

**Exits:** Echo Pens, Processional Court, Hall of Measures.

### C2 — Echo Pens

**Arrival:** Missing livestock and two lost scouts stand silent in translucent stalls. At each second toll they complete one tiny part of entering the pen.

**Interactables / discoverables**

- Bronze collar rail feeding the castle mechanism.
- Scout's report describing windows that replay different centuries.
- Tamsin's ram, which can be led out by removing its collar between tolls.

**Rescue:** Removing the master tithe key stops new collections. Freeing everyone requires three successful actions timed between tolls, no check if the party understands the rule. Hasty attempts call the Courtyard Sentinels.

**Benefit:** Rescued scouts explain the safe court route. Freeing the animals removes the Bell Regent's 10 temporary HP at the start of combat.

### C3 — Processional Court

**Arrival:** Armored patrols cross the same courtyard in incompatible eras. A dry fountain throws water upward on every replay.

#### Combat: Courtyard Sentinels

**Trigger:** Crossing without the restored captain's cadence, harming a replay, or tripping the Echo Pens alarm.

**Enemies**

- 2 Animated Armors, each reduced to 25 HP.
- 1 Processional Echo: AC 13, HP 18, +4 glaive for 1d8+2 force; cannot take reactions.

**Terrain / mechanic**

- Three patrol lines are visibly marked. On initiative 10, spectral guards charge along the last occupied line; DC 12 Dexterity save or 1d8 force and knocked prone.
- The reversing fountain grants half cover when “falling” and pushes creatures 5 feet when replaying upward.
- Showing Bryn's captain token or speaking the patrol cadence from the scout report prevents the Processional Echo from joining.

**Tactics**

- Armors block exits and shove targets into patrol lines.
- The Echo charges in a straight line and can hit the armors.
- Armors stop fighting if both are moved onto their original plinths at the second toll.

**Scaling**

- Easier: one armor begins on 15 HP.
- Harder: use full Animated Armor HP; do not add another enemy.

**Reward / clue**

- Captain's coffer: 45 gp, two potions of healing, and a silvered shortsword.
- Mural shows castle builders installing bells outward through farms, proving town infrastructure was part of the design.

### C4 — Hall of Measures

**Arrival:** A long gallery of rulers, plumb lines, metronomes, and portraits whose subjects repeat different gestures. The exit door has no handle on this side.

**Discoverables**

- **Builder's Mural:** Workers build the castle around a black absence already in the hill. Purpose: shows the anomaly predates the castle.
- **Three Door Sketches:** Three copies show the same freestanding doorway with different surroundings and proportions. Purpose: ambiguous long-term foreshadowing only; never label it Threshold in player data.
- **Caldris Portrait:** Ser Caldris Veyr wearing the same chest bell as the Regent. Purpose: provides the boss's human name and a roleplay advantage.
- `VII` is carved beside “response interval,” not a room number.

**Checks**

- DC 12 Investigation identifies a six-second repeating sequence among the portraits.
- DC 13 Arcana determines the room measures recurrence, not time travel.
- DC 14 History extracts Caldris's name and voluntary oath from damaged captions.
- DC 15 Perception spots the inconsistent doorway drawings. The baseline mural remains visible on any failure.

**Puzzle: the unfinished gesture**

Four statues perform reach, turn, kneel, and release. Players must perform any three, then let their Second Motions complete the fourth while they stand clear. Avra's litany gives the sequence. Brute force, Knock, or 20 cumulative damage opens the door but awakens two Script Wraiths in C5.

**Exits:** Ward Archive and Banquet of Last Duties.

### C5 — Ward Archive

**Arrival:** Metal leaves hang like pages. Each spoken word stamps itself onto a leaf six seconds later, sometimes in an older hand.

**Required records, available without a check**

- The hill anomaly existed before Castle Veyr.
- Builders created an **anchor heart** to constrain it into a predictable pattern.
- Ser Caldris entered the mechanism willingly as its first keeper.
- The system tracked other unnamed “stations” capable of answering.

**Checks for advantages**

- DC 12 Arcana: anchor bells lose resistance when struck by their own recorded force.
- DC 13 Investigation: the current collection cycle is reclaiming distributed bell bronze.
- DC 14 Religion: reciting Caldris's full oath can interrupt one Regent lair action.
- DC 15 History: locate the sealed `VII / RESPONSE` leaf and the Lornwatch routing strip.

**Optional combat: Script Wraiths**

Triggered only if the Hall door was forced or a player deliberately tears out a record.

- 2 Specters reduced to 18 HP; damage appears as letters erased from the target's shadow.
- At initiative 10, the last verbal spell cast repeats at its minimum level with the caster choosing a legal target; if no spell was cast, the last spoken name summons a harmless but distracting aftervoice.
- Reward: scroll of **Silence** and advantage on the next archive check.

**DM-only note:** The archive never states who created the first anomaly, how many stations exist, or anything about the world's true nature.

### C6 — Banquet of Last Duties

**Arrival:** An intact feast decays, restores, and is served again in six-second fragments. Transparent servants try to seat intruders under old nameplates.

**Roleplay / environmental challenge**

- A lucid afterimage of Caldris's daughter, **Elowen**, asks whether duty still counts as love when no one remembers choosing it.
- She knows her father volunteered and that the builders feared “an answer from beyond the road.” She does not know what answered.
- Treating her as a person earns the **Caldris Oathline** automatically. Dismissing or attacking her releases a concussive replay (DC 12 Constitution save, 2d6 thunder) but the party can still read the oath from a nameplate.

**Loot**

- **Echo-Step Brooch** (uncommon, requires attunement): once per long rest, as a bonus action return to an unoccupied space you occupied since the end of your previous turn, up to 30 feet away. This is teleportation and does not provoke opportunity attacks.
- 60 gp in preserved table silver.
- A spell scroll of **Misty Step** for Theldren or another compatible party use.

**Exit:** Pendulum stair to the Bellfoundry.

### C7 — Bellfoundry Undercroft

**Arrival:** Molten-looking bronze flows uphill while stolen livestock bells hammer themselves into three massive anchor bells.

#### Combat: the Recasting

**Trigger:** The party tries to stop the bronze flow, takes an anchor tool, or reaches the central gantry.

**Enemies**

- 1 Bronze Ram: use Dire Wolf statistics, AC 15, damage becomes bludgeoning, no Pack Tactics, and its charge knocks prone on a failed DC 12 Strength save.
- 2 Foundry Refrains: AC 12, HP 14, +4 touch for 1d6+2 fire; immune to fire, vulnerable to thunder.

**Terrain / mechanic**

- Two moving pour-channels replay their path on initiative 10; visible orange light marks both routes. Crossing a route then deals 2d6 fire, DC 12 Dexterity for half.
- A hanging mold can be released with an action or ranged attack (AC 12), dealing 2d8 bludgeoning in a 5-foot square and cooling one channel.
- Ringing Pipp's tuning key, a recovered bell, or the Silence spell disables one Foundry Refrain for a round.

**Tactics**

- The Ram charges across gantries and tries to knock targets into a pour route.
- Refrains herd characters rather than focus damage.
- The Ram attacks a visible Second Motion if baited, potentially destroying a channel control.

**Scaling**

- Easier: one Foundry Refrain and 2d4 fire channels.
- Harder: Ram has 45 HP and regains 5 HP once when a bell reaches the forge.

**Reward / clue**

- **Anchor Hammer:** mundane warhammer that counts as magical while inside this Hollow; it deals double damage to anchor bells.
- Nessa's missing master mold, clearing or confirming her role depending on party choices.
- Control board can free any creatures left in the Echo Pens.

**Exits:** Regent's Belfry; service stair back to C1.

### C8 — Regent's Belfry

**Arrival:** A high circular chamber with no quadrants or marked blast zones. A giant pendulum passes through the floor without touching it. Three anchor bells hang at different heights. Ser Caldris's bronze-bound figure turns only after the party has already moved, copying their posture a beat late.

#### Catalyst encounter: the Bell Regent

**Trigger:** The Catalyst Fragment enters the chamber, the party strikes an anchor bell, or anyone addresses Caldris by name. The Regent says, “Seventh response received. Positions will be preserved.”

**Bell Regent profile**

- Medium construct, AC 15, HP 72, speed 30.
- Saves: Constitution +5, Wisdom +3. Resistant to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing while two or more anchor bells remain.
- **Multiattack:** two Bellstaff attacks, +5 to hit, 1d8+3 bludgeoning.
- **Command the Refrain (recharge 5–6):** immediately trigger one visible recorded attack or movement.
- **Toll of Assignment (once at half HP):** DC 13 Wisdom save; on failure, speed becomes 0 until the creature uses its action to reject the assigned position. Pipp's key or Caldris's oath grants advantage.

**Three anchor bells**

- Each bell: AC 13, 14 HP, immune to psychic and poison, resistant to ordinary weapon damage, vulnerable to thunder and **replayed** damage.
- For each bell destroyed, the Regent loses one benefit in order: physical resistance after the first, Command the Refrain after the second, then 1 AC after the third (to AC 14).
- A replay that hits a bell deals double damage. The Anchor Hammer also deals double damage.

**Lair rhythm**

- **Initiative 20 — Record:** Mark each creature's current space and the line or target of its most recent attack, spell, or 10+ feet of movement. Use visible echo markers.
- **Initiative 10 — Second Motion:** Each marked action repeats once. Replayed weapon attacks use +4 and deal 1d6+2 force; replayed damaging spells become a 5-foot burst for 1d6 force (DC 12 Dexterity half); replayed movement travels the same line and pushes a creature it crosses 5 feet (DC 12 Strength negates). A creature can move away, aim its earlier line at a bell, or deliberately record a useful action.
- Do not replay healing, conditions, spell-slot use, or resource costs. The mechanic is positioning and intent, not bookkeeping punishment.

**Terrain / mechanics**

- Hanging chains allow DC 11 Athletics swings between platforms.
- The pendulum supplies moving half cover on initiative 5 and pushes rather than damages on contact.
- Three raised platforms put bells within melee reach; stairs and chains make all reachable.
- A character adjacent to the control lectern can use an action and the Caldris Oathline to cancel the next Initiative 10 replay once.

**Tactics**

- The Regent stands where player attacks will leave dangerous recorded lines, then moves before replay.
- It shoves rather than pursues a character already lined up with an anchor bell.
- It prioritizes the Fragment carrier only until called Caldris; then it loses its next reaction.
- At 20 HP, human speech breaks through: “If I stop, what keeps the answer out?” This is tragedy, not a reason the party must spare the Catalyst.

**Alternate solutions**

- Destroy all three bells and reduce the Regent to 0 HP.
- Use the full seventh verse at the lectern, place Pipp's tuning key in the control slot, and survive three replay cycles; Caldris then asks the party to break the heart. It still ends with the Catalyst destroyed.
- A creative collapse of the pendulum onto the heart deals 25 damage to the Regent and all bells but forces DC 12 Dexterity saves for 2d6 bludgeoning, half on success.

**Scaling**

- Easier: Regent 58 HP, one Bellstaff attack on round 1, anchor bells 10 HP.
- Harder: Regent 88 HP and one Foundry Refrain arrives when the first bell breaks.
- If the party enters depleted, Elowen's afterimage grants each character 5 temporary HP and suppresses the first Initiative 10 replay.

**Reward / clue**

- **Veyrholt Catalyst Shard:** story item that resonates with the Greymere fragment but has no required mechanics yet.
- 120 gp civic reward, the Echo-Step Brooch if not already found, and the town's Wayfarer Writ.
- Archive truth: this Catalyst was constructed to give an older Hollow a predictable form.

## After the Catalyst

### Immediate consequences

- Every recorded action in Castle Veyr performs once in harmless chronological order: builders lift stones, families dine, guards change, Caldris removes his crown, and thousands of bells ring together.
- The Bell Regent becomes briefly human in outline, thanks the party for allowing his duty to end, and collapses into inert bronze.
- The silver boundary folds inward. Castle windows show only the current ruined rooms.
- All Veyrholt ward bells crack along the same seven-line fracture. Their R2/storage art should show this without adding explanatory text.
- Missing livestock and scouts become fully present. No Supabase or story asset migration occurs as part of this event; that restriction concerns real infrastructure, not fiction.
- Roads and farms remain ordinary and usable. Veyrholt must replace folk procedures that had quietly depended on the Hollow's predictability.

### The significant revelation

The Ward Archive proves that builders found an existing anomaly and deliberately created an anchor heart around a willing keeper. Over time that anchor became the Catalyst sustaining Veyrholt's patterned Hollow. **Catalysts can be constructed, and at least some were intended as restraints.**

This answers why Veyrholt could coexist with its Hollow. It does not answer who first understood Hollows, what the stations ultimately contain, why `VII` recurs, or whether breaking Greymere woke Veyrholt.

### The unsettling correlation

When both Catalyst fragments are placed near the Reeve's clock ledger, the Greymere fragment pulses six seconds before the Veyrholt shard. The ledger's first double toll is timestamped to the same minute as the Greymere victory. Present three possibilities through NPC debate:

- destroying Greymere sent a signal;
- Veyrholt reacted to the same unknown event;
- someone or something deliberately synchronized them.

Do not confirm one during this chapter.

### Choice and town consequences

- **Nessa exposed harshly:** She loses the bellworks; Veyrholt rewards the party but future ward repairs are slower.
- **Nessa required to make restitution:** She remains under Reeve oversight and begins recasting civic bells from ordinary bronze. This is the most stable result.
- **Nessa protected without accountability:** Town trust drops; Bryn remains suspicious of the party.
- **Kest turned in:** Bryn respects the lawfulness, but the party loses a useful under-road contact.
- **Kest spared for honest testimony:** Kest becomes a future smuggler contact and returns the unrelated lamb.
- **Archive preserved:** Avra can provide research later. If destroyed, the Lornwatch clue survives as a stamped metal strip on the Catalyst frame.

### Level and rewards

- Advance the party to **Level 4** after a safe rest following the Catalyst's destruction.
- Veyrholt pays 120 gp plus the 40 gp road reward if not already collected.
- **Wayfarer's Writ:** free modest lodging and mundane resupply in Veyrholt, plus advantage on the first social check with civic officials who recognize its seal.
- Keep both Catalyst fragments as theatre-of-the-mind story objects, notes, items, or discoverables; do not invent a broad fragment-power system yet.

## Hook to Chapter 3: Lornwatch

Six seconds after Castle Veyr's last bell falls silent, a low bell answers from far to the east though the day is clear and the distance is impossible. The Ward Archive stamps a final line onto a blank strip:

> LORNWATCH — WATCHER ABSENT — RESPONSE ACCEPTED

Mara Venn knows Lornwatch Abbey as an abandoned observatory-monastery on the Glass Road and says a Varakai observer was supposed to remain there. The route strip shows someone departed Lornwatch for Veyrholt three days before the Greymere Catalyst fell, but no such traveler arrived.

This gives the party a location, a missing person, and a reason for urgency without explaining the network or the campaign's final truth.

## DM pacing and rests

- Allow a long rest after Bellpost Road before arrival; this is when Level 3 takes effect.
- Veyrholt investigation is one flexible adventuring day. A long rest at the Brass Lamb is safe but advances the tithe: one additional flock vanishes and the castle gains 10 temporary HP unless the Pens are freed.
- Allow one short rest in the Ward Archive or Banquet Hall.
- Avoid more than three mandatory combats inside the castle. Courtyard can be bypassed, Script Wraiths are optional, and the culvert can change encounter order.
- Use a town or dungeon incident whenever momentum slows, but do not punish careful roleplay with constant combat.
