# Mossjaw Cavern — Ankheg infestation

DM ONLY. Replaces the unplayed mother/cub quest at the DM's request, 2026-10-15. This ordinary livestock infestation has no Hollow connection. Preserve the existing scene, links, token coordinates and all campaign progress. Retain retired creature records and the pre-change snapshot as history; never restore an old campaign snapshot over play.

Oren Bramble wants giant burrowing insects eliminated and offers **40 gp** for clearing both. Farmers report animals dragged underground, fresh tunnels and acid-damaged remains. Oren knows the safe stream trail and farm reports, not the creatures' exact numbers. He is meticulous, concerned and helpful; practical questions earn directions, threats end credit, unexpected plans get sensible supply advice. The DM roleplays him. His ten-item SHOP remains unchanged.

The notice at Bramble & Brass supplies the offer and trail automatically. Survival DC11 identifies drag furrows and fresh burrowing activity; failure costs time, never the route. Nature DC12 recognizes acid-spitting insects; their tunnels, shed chitin and pitted bones telegraph the threat regardless. No cub, nest, relocation dilemma, corruption marks or secret mastermind.

## Encounter and terrain

Two Large Ankhegs, each **AC14, HP39, walk30ft, burrow10ft**; STR17 DEX11 CON13 INT1 WIS13 CHA6, darkvision60ft, tremorsense60ft. One begins visible in the central clearing; the other is hidden at the northeast burrow. Use their existing two scene tokens and separate monster instances, not Deploy encounter (which would add another pair).

Roll each creature into initiative separately with the native token control. On round2, telegraph soil shaking, reveal the second with its token's Show to players control, then add it to initiative. No extra surprise round. Revealing is entirely DM-controlled; no scripted event. If caution draws it out before combat, reveal early.

- **Bite:** melee +5, reach5ft, one target; 2d6+3 slashing plus 1d6 acid. On hit grapple a Large-or-smaller target, escape DC13. Until released, bite only that target, with advantage. DM applies Grappled and adjudicates escape using existing controls.
- **Acid Spray (Recharge6):** 30ft line,5ft wide; DC13DEX,3d6 acid, half on success. Cannot spray while grappling. Roll d6 at the start of its turn after use; only6 restores it. Track each creature's recharge independently. Native action cards provide attack/damage rolls and show save/recharge; the DM places the line and resolves saves/conditions.
- Stream is difficult terrain; boulders give half cover; northeast shelf10ft high, DC10Athletics to climb quickly, safe longer path without a roll. Burrow only through soft earth, never limestone. Do not drag a PC into an inaccessible off-map tunnel. Exits and retreat remain usable.

For four Level4 characters this is an upper-medium 2014 encounter: two CR2 creatures,900XP base,1350 adjusted, below the1500 hard threshold. Reveal in stages; line attacks normally threaten one or two PCs, never force the whole party into a line. Offer a rest before/after. If already depleted, delay the second spray and entrance rather than surprise-killing PCs. No attacks on downed PCs and no reinforcements. This is a proper fight, not a mandatory animal-welfare choice. Clever barriers, bait or excavation may earn advantage or remove cover; eliminating both resolves the infestation.
