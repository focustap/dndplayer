## 01 — Sunpetal Square / opening arrest

Arrival: painted shutters, sunlit pale stone, orange and blue market awnings, green trees, flower baskets, fountain and ordinary families. Two guards in a town that otherwise barely seems to need a watch. The town is thriving; fear is social, not mind control. The memorial stands along the north garden.

DM read-aloud option (never app dialogue): A man picks a public flower for his crying daughter. A guard announces, **“Municipal Ordinance 14. Unauthorized removal of public property. Seven days' imprisonment.”** He tries to run. **His arms are violently pulled behind his back by an invisible force and immobilized. No manacles appear.** The guards barely react. People resume their cheerful shopping. Nobody helps. Baseline weirdness and injustice are automatic, with no roll.

Optional DM voices: merchant, “Our laws keep Lornwatch beautiful!” Resident, “Welcome to Lornwatch! We hope you enjoy your stay. It's the safest town in Elyndra!” then quietly, “Just make sure you read the notices.” A woman whispers, “He was always such a good father.” Use only if helpful; roleplay freely.

Father: Jory Reed; daughter: Nell. Intervene: guards first explain the posted law and offer an immediate hearing, never attack for a question. Persuasion DC 13 wins an escort without confinement; failure means supervised courthouse attendance, not blocked progress. Violent escalation: two guards defend and disengage toward the courthouse, accept surrender, never execute fallen characters. No unavoidable town brawl. Ilan's restraint is a scene demonstration, not an unlimited ability to disable PCs. In combat use only his written temporary restrictions.

Ignore arrest: Nell goes to Mira at the Golden Finch; registry records Jory's detention. Follow guards, read notices, help daughter, request hearing or inspect records: all lead onward. A spouse stifles a protest (Insight/Persuasion DC 15 earns the family's trust); uncomfortable guard (DC 13, earns late-night stair information); whispered sympathy (DC 10, points to inn). Failed checks get public directions and visible discomfort, never remove routes.

Jory is an embarrassed, protective working father, wants Nell safe, and knows only the posted charge; he conceals no plot secret. Calm help earns cooperation, threats make him put Nell behind him, an unexpected escape plan gets practical concern for where she will sleep. Nell wants her father back, knows the inn serves warm bread, and has no supernatural insight; reassurance works better than interrogation. Guards are tired local employees, want an orderly hearing, know only ordinances and patrol routes, and fear being charged themselves; they hide misgivings, not knowledge of the sphere. Legal challenges get an escort, kindness can earn stair timing, intimidation draws witnesses, and unexpected solutions go to the clerk rather than triggering automatic violence.

Objects: ordinance board (automatic inspection); Tomas memorial inscription (public, no VII); fountain ribbon with lost brass button (Investigation DC 11, return for a pastry). This last find is ordinary town life. Exits: every town interior and Glass Road; cave trail via general store. Reward for helping Nell: free supper, not compulsory money.

Warm saffron walls, teal cushions, open hearth, hanging flowers, clean guest rooms. Mira at counter; Nell appears only if Jory was detained. Show a safe place before sinister details accumulate.

Mira Fen — host, practical and protective, likes knowing who has eaten. Wants guests safe and Nell reunited. Knows rooms have emptied after petty arrests, Ilan once paid fines from his own pocket, clerks accept written petitions. Does not know a sphere or Hollow exists. Conceals a spare room for frightened families. Friendly questions get food and basic directions; accusations in public make her lower her voice and offer a private table; threats cause her to protect Nell and call for witnesses; unexpected offers of help become errands or introductions, never an exposition dump. Can grant a safe rest and introduce a clerk. Never announces the source of the laws.

Menu/shop: supper 1 gp, bed 1 gp, private room 2 gp, tea 1 gp, ration bundle 1 gp. Prices are app inventory; accommodation and rest are adjudicated by DM. Nell knows only that her father wanted her to stop crying. No required check. Guest ledger physical clue records three lodgings ended by detention and directs petitions to courthouse registry. Perception DC 12 notices an untouched packed bag; returning it earns 5 gp and family goodwill, unrelated to Hollow.

Exits: square. Rest before basement is available; no arbitrary doom clock for taking it. Ilan may call a hearing if party ignores all other leads.

## 03 — Honeycrust Bakery

She knows Ilan once forgave a poor family's fine and now barely listens; she knows no supernatural explanation.

Exits square/courthouse by DM travel.

## 05 — Ada's cottage and Tomas memorial

Small rose-painted cottage, blue door, lush herb garden, bright kitchen with tea and two family chairs. Ada Rook — warm elderly gardener; Ilan and late Tomas's mother. Wants her family remembered and Ilan to visit. Knows his childhood, loss, former kindness and last visits; does NOT know of the sphere, Hollow, basement worship or true Catalyst. Hides grief and worry behind hospitality, not complicity.

FIRST VISIT: tea, gardening, practical town directions; Ilan is busy. She avoids a life-story monologue. Friendly help earns a reason to return. Demands or accusations make her defend the son she remembers; she may end visit, but never erase other investigation routes. Unanticipated kindness (mending fence, sharing a memory, helping Nell) earns trust, not an automatic reveal. Insight DC12 recognizes grief but does not unlock a script.

DM-MANAGED REVEAL conditions, private notes only:

- Memorial route: after initial investigation, have Ada lay a **white flower at Tomas's memorial**. Guards challenge her under the SAME Ordinance 14. She is shocked her son's law threatens her for honoring Tomas. Whether PCs intervene, accompany her to hearing, or visit after release, she can now share her full story.
- Alternate route: PCs find Tomas's old case at registry and ask Ada about it. This also earns the story; no memorial scene required.
- The DM chooses timing, form and length. No automatic dialogue unlock, checkbox schema, player marker, or obligatory conversation choices.

Full private story reference / optional read-aloud, adapted freely:

“Tomas was killed outside a tavern. There were people who saw what happened, but the evidence was mishandled. The man who killed him walked free. Ilan couldn't bear that. He became a lawyer, then a judge, because he wanted the law to protect people who couldn't protect themselves. He used to pay poor people's fines himself. He'd come here every Sunday with flowers, mend whatever I'd broken, and tell me about someone he'd helped. Then he started coming late. He looked so tired. He said he'd found something that could change everything. After a while he stopped visiting. The last time, he told me: **‘Mother, I finally understand why Tomas died. I thought the world had failed him. But the world was simply incomplete. Something has shown me how to fix it.’** I don't know what he meant. **If you see him, tell him his mother misses him.**”

If asked beyond her knowledge, Ada admits uncertainty; never make her infer the sphere. If escorted below by choice, Ilan hesitates for one action, once; distant mention of her love can have same effect with convincing personal detail. No roll required after earned story. She need not enter danger. Talk cannot destroy the sphere. Do not punish players who skip her: basement counterplay remains available. Garden find: pressed flower / mundane childhood drawing, only shown by Ada's consent; no illicit exposition inside a random drawer. Exits square.

## 06 — Courthouse hearing, registry and cells

Airy cream stone, tall windows, green benches, polished wood, public petition desk; competent clerks and few guards. Ilan appears clean and courteous at a hearing, later disheveled below. Basement does not appear on public town maps as a marked secret destination.

Wants compliance and validation; knows the sphere exists and amplifies his personal rule sorcery; mistakes it for an all-seeing divine Witness. Doesn't know its origin, VII meaning or campaign truth. Conceals worship below and moral deterioration. Polite questions get a patient hearing and faulty proportionality; a good objection can release one detainee under supervision; public humiliation makes him legalistic; violence makes him defend the court; unexpected mercy or Tomas/Ada details can briefly reach him. Wants records preserved and people released, knows service stairs, hours Ilan vanishes, increasing sentences and pressure from floor below; knows no sphere. Hides a service key under blotter, not essential progress. Courtesy gets public files automatically; DC12 Persuasion gets key/safe timing; failed roll means supervised access to cells and stair landing. Threats prompt her to secure originals but duplicate files survive. Unexpected practical offers can earn escort or preservation of testimony. This returns the Veyrholt report to its recipient without implying an abbey.

## 07 — Basement encounter: Ilan and the sphere

Optional private introductory read-aloud, never automatically displayed:

You have to see it. It's beautiful. Something beyond our world. Beyond the gods themselves. It sees everything. Every mistake we've ever made. Enemy stats: Ilan AC15 HP90 speed30, STR10 DEX12 CON14 INT16 WIS12 CHA16, saves WIS+3 CHA+5; one action/turn, **Judicial force** two bolts +5 ranged spell 60ft, each 1d6+3 force, normal adjacent-enemy disadvantage. Sphere AC13 HP60 speed0, no attacks, no turns except tracked initiative marker if useful; immune poison/psychic damage and conditions, ordinary weapon/spell damage otherwise works.
