## 01 — Sunpetal Square / opening arrest

Arrival: painted shutters, sunlit pale stone, orange and blue market awnings, green trees, flower baskets, fountain and ordinary families. Two guards in a town that otherwise barely seems to need a watch. The town is thriving; fear is social, not mind control. The memorial stands along the north garden.

DM read-aloud option (never app dialogue): A man picks a public flower for his crying daughter. A guard announces, **“Municipal Ordinance 14. Unauthorized removal of public property. Seven days' imprisonment.”** He tries to run. **His arms are violently pulled behind his back by an invisible force and immobilized. No manacles appear.** The guards barely react. People resume their cheerful shopping. Nobody helps. Baseline weirdness and injustice are automatic, with no roll.

Optional DM voices: merchant, “Our laws keep Lornwatch beautiful!” Resident, “Welcome to Lornwatch! We hope you enjoy your stay. It's the safest town in Elyndra!” then quietly, “Just make sure you read the notices.” A woman whispers, “He was always such a good father.” Use only if helpful; roleplay freely.

Father: Jory Reed; daughter: Nell. Intervene: guards first explain the posted law and offer an immediate hearing, never attack for a question. Persuasion DC 13 wins an escort without confinement; failure means supervised courthouse attendance, not blocked progress. Violent escalation: two guards defend and disengage toward the courthouse, accept surrender, never execute fallen characters. No unavoidable town brawl. Ilan's restraint is a scene demonstration, not an unlimited ability to disable PCs. In combat use only his written temporary restrictions.

Ignore arrest: Nell goes to Mira at the Golden Finch; registry records Jory's detention. Follow guards, read notices, help daughter, request hearing or inspect records: all lead onward. A spouse stifles a protest (Insight/Persuasion DC 15 earns the family's trust); uncomfortable guard (DC 13, earns late-night stair information); whispered sympathy (DC 10, points to inn). Failed checks get public directions and visible discomfort, never remove routes.

Objects: ordinance board (automatic inspection); Tomas memorial inscription (public, no VII); fountain ribbon with lost brass button (Investigation DC 11, return for a pastry). This last find is ordinary town life. Exits: every town interior and Glass Road; cave trail via general store. Reward for helping Nell: free supper, not compulsory money.

## 02 — Golden Finch Inn

Warm saffron walls, teal cushions, open hearth, hanging flowers, clean guest rooms. Mira at counter; Nell appears only if Jory was detained. Show a safe place before sinister details accumulate.

Mira Fen — host, practical and protective, likes knowing who has eaten. Wants guests safe and Nell reunited. Knows rooms have emptied after petty arrests, Ilan once paid fines from his own pocket, clerks accept written petitions. Does not know a sphere or Hollow exists. Conceals a spare room for frightened families. Friendly questions get food and basic directions; accusations in public make her lower her voice and offer a private table; threats cause her to protect Nell and call for witnesses; unexpected offers of help become errands or introductions, never an exposition dump. Can grant a safe rest and introduce a clerk. Never announces the source of the laws.

Menu/shop: supper 0.5 gp, bed 0.5 gp, private room 2 gp, tea 0.1 gp, ration bundle 0.5 gp. Prices are app inventory; accommodation and rest are adjudicated by DM. Nell knows only that her father wanted her to stop crying. No required check. Guest ledger physical clue records three lodgings ended by detention and directs petitions to courthouse registry. Perception DC 12 notices an untouched packed bag; returning it earns 5 gp and family goodwill, unrelated to Hollow.

Exits: square. Rest before basement is available; no arbitrary doom clock for taking it. Ilan may call a hearing if party ignores all other leads.

## 03 — Honeycrust Bakery

Butter-yellow shop, copper trays, berries and cooling pies; small flour-dusted kitchen. Martha Bell is in holding cells, not magically present at the bakery. Her adult granddaughter Pippa Bell keeps baking and paying rent.

Pippa — brisk, sleep-deprived, stubbornly hospitable. Wants Martha home and business intact. Knows the sentence for burned pies, date of arrest, and which clerk accepted the appeal. Doesn't know why laws bind people or anything underground. Hides unpaid bills, not a cult secret. Empathy earns a copy of the sentence without a roll; legal precision earns a signed petition; intimidation makes her insist on witnesses; unexpected plans receive practical limits (cannot leave oven long, can identify grandmother). She can supply food for cave relocation. Martha in cells is outspoken, proud of recipes, and ashamed people had to help. She knows Ilan once forgave a poor family's fine and now barely listens; she knows no supernatural explanation.

Physical sentencing order: Martha Bell, prohibited sale of overcooked public-market goods, fourteen days; appeal at registry. No essential clue gated by a check. Investigation DC 12 compares an older warning with this prison term, granting advantage on one hearing appeal. SHOP: berry bun 0.1 gp, bread 0.2 gp, pie 1 gp, livestock lure scraps 0.2 gp. Free stale crusts if PCs help. After sphere defeat, release is a civic decision documented by clerk; DM reunites family. Reward: 10 gp contributed by neighbors and a week's bread, once only. Exits square/courthouse by DM travel.

## 04 — Bramble & Brass / Mossjaw quest

Green-painted shelves, brass fittings, colorful rope and camping gear. Oren Bramble is a mundane merchant, not a magic-item vendor.

Oren — meticulous, good-natured, worried about livestock bills. Wants losses stopped without harming nearby farms. Knows claw marks, missing goats, safe cave trail; does not know there is a cub until tracks are examined, and knows nothing about the sphere. Conceals that he has already paid two farmers. Buyers get fair prices; wildlife expertise earns full trust; threats end credit, not the trail; unexpected peaceful proposals earn supplies lent against return. He will pay **40 gp for ending livestock losses**, including relocation or secured animal pens, not just a corpse.

SHOP: rope 1 gp, torch 0.01 gp, rations 0.5 gp, waterskin 0.2 gp, crowbar 2 gp, shovel 2 gp, hunting trap 5 gp, healer's kit 5 gp (2), ball bearings 1 gp, sack 0.01 gp. Nothing magical. Physical livestock notice includes tracks, offer and trail directions. Survival DC 11 identifies adult + smaller tracks; on failure cub is visible on entering cave. No Hollow marks, corruption or secret mastermind.

Cave: green ravine, mossy limestone mouth, shallow stream, upper shelf with nesting hollow. Adult owlbear Large, AC13 HP59, speed40; cub Small AC11 HP10 speed25, noncombatant. Encounter begins only if cornered, nest threatened, or attacked. Adult multiattack beak +7 1d10+5 piercing and claws +7 2d8+5 slashing; retreats to cub when bloodied; never pursues beyond ravine. Keen Sight/Smell advantage on relevant Perception. Cub hides, never attacks. Stream difficult terrain, boulders half cover, shelf 10 ft high (Athletics DC10; safe longer path automatic). Keep exits usable.

Peaceful resolution: observe from cover, offer substantial meat away from farms, then guide to unoccupied quarry above ravine or reinforce farm pens. Animal Handling DC13 (advantage with druid assistance/food) buys calm approach; failed roll causes warning charge, time loss and retreat opportunity, not forced combat. Speak with Animals applies only if table's rules permit monstrosities; mundane behavior and appropriate magic can still help. Two meaningful efforts resolve relocation; no repeated single-check lottery. Killing mother leaves orphan welfare decision; reward same for secured farms, consequences social not punitive. Search abandoned smuggler satchel off nest: 12 gp, potion of healing, ordinary silver brooch 8 gp; Survival DC12 finds shortcut to it, careful search automatic. Easy: adult HP45 and one attack/turn. Hard only for fresh confident party: HP65, unchanged offense. No second adult or attacking cub. Allow safe rest afterward. Return to square/store.

## 05 — Ada's cottage and Tomas memorial

Small rose-painted cottage, blue door, lush herb garden, bright kitchen with tea and two family chairs. Ada Rook — warm elderly gardener; Ilan and late Tomas's mother. Wants her family remembered and Ilan to visit. Knows his childhood, loss, former kindness and last visits; does NOT know of the sphere, Hollow, basement worship or true Catalyst. Hides grief and worry behind hospitality, not complicity.

FIRST VISIT: tea, gardening, practical town directions; Ilan is busy. She avoids a life-story monologue. Friendly help earns a reason to return. Demands or accusations make her defend the son she remembers; she may end visit, but never erase other investigation routes. Unanticipated kindness (mending fence, sharing a memory, helping Nell) earns trust, not an automatic reveal. Insight DC12 recognizes grief but does not unlock a script.

DM-MANAGED REVEAL conditions, private notes only:

- Memorial route: after initial investigation, have Ada lay a **white flower at Tomas's memorial**. Guards challenge her under the SAME Ordinance 14. She is shocked her son's law threatens her for honoring Tomas. Whether PCs intervene, accompany her to hearing, or visit after release, she can now share her full story.
- Alternate route: PCs find Tomas's old case at registry and ask Ada about it. This also earns the story; no memorial scene required.
- The DM chooses timing, form and length. No automatic dialogue unlock, checkbox schema, player marker, or obligatory conversation choices.

Full private story reference / optional read-aloud, adapted freely:

“Tomas was killed outside a tavern. There were people who saw what happened, but the evidence was mishandled. The man who killed him walked free. Ilan couldn't bear that. He became a lawyer, then a judge, because he wanted the law to protect people who couldn't protect themselves. He used to pay poor people's fines himself. He'd come here every Sunday with flowers, mend whatever I'd broken, and tell me about someone he'd helped. Then he started coming late. He looked so tired. He said he'd found something that could change everything. After a while he stopped visiting. The last time, he told me: **‘Mother, I finally understand why Tomas died. I thought the world had failed him. But the world was simply incomplete. Something has shown me how to fix it.’** I don't know what he meant. **If you see him, tell him his mother misses him.**”

If asked beyond her knowledge, Ada admits uncertainty; never make her infer the sphere. If escorted below by choice, Ilan hesitates for one action, once; distant mention of her love can have same effect with convincing personal detail. No roll required after earned story. She need not enter danger. Talk cannot destroy the sphere. Do not punish players who skip her: basement counterplay remains available. Garden find: pressed flower / mundane childhood drawing, only shown by Ada's consent; no illicit exposition inside a random drawer. Exits square.

## 06 — Courthouse hearing, registry and cells

Airy cream stone, tall windows, green benches, polished wood, public petition desk; competent clerks and few guards. Ilan appears clean and courteous at a hearing, later disheveled below. Basement does not appear on public town maps as a marked secret destination.

Ilan Rook — brilliant judge, once compassionate, now convinced perfect enforcement will prevent another Tomas. Wants compliance and validation; knows the sphere exists and amplifies his personal rule sorcery; mistakes it for an all-seeing divine Witness. Doesn't know its origin, VII meaning or campaign truth. Conceals worship below and moral deterioration. Polite questions get a patient hearing and faulty proportionality; a good objection can release one detainee under supervision; public humiliation makes him legalistic; violence makes him defend the court; unexpected mercy or Tomas/Ada details can briefly reach him. Never supplies reliable cosmology. Ordinary magic once bound voluntary agreements/legitimate judgments; mass reality law is recent corruption.

Clerk Sera Pell — missing surveyor was detained as a records witness; she now works under supervision at registry. Precise, anxious, keeps duplicate records. Wants records preserved and people released, knows service stairs, hours Ilan vanishes, increasing sentences and pressure from floor below; knows no sphere. Hides a service key under blotter, not essential progress. Courtesy gets public files automatically; DC12 Persuasion gets key/safe timing; failed roll means supervised access to cells and stair landing. Threats prompt her to secure originals but duplicate files survive. Unexpected practical offers can earn escort or preservation of testimony. This returns the Veyrholt report to its recipient without implying an abbey.

Evidence objects, all readable without checks once inspecting registry:

1. Ordinance comparison: three months ago public flower removal meant restitution; today seven days. Confirms escalation. Investigation DC12 identifies dates clustering around excavation.
2. Tomas case: killed near tavern; acquittal because chain of custody failed; Ilan was a grieving relative, not presiding judge. Opens Ada alternate trigger.
3. Civic survey report: recent cavity beneath courthouse; after-hours inspections signed I.R.; stair service route drawn on page. Direct route below even without Ada/bakery/cave. Arcana DC13 notices compass deflection annotation; advantage on first restriction save, not an origin answer.
4. Arrest register: Jory, Martha, surveyor witness detention; location holding cells. A DC12 legal/persuasion appeal wins one temporary release; failures earn a written rejection and permission to visit, keeping progress.

In cells, invisible restraint slackens briefly when a deep vibration interrupts Ilan's concentration. Clerks or detainees can point to stairs. If party ignores documents, Ilan adjourns downstairs while floor pressure makes paper slide toward the service door. Pursue, obtain escort, use key, or take ten minutes to loosen old latch. Thieves' tools DC13 saves time; failure draws clerk, who can escort; door never permanently blocks plot. Hidden link is a DM control; reveal basement scene only now. Guard nonlethal alternative: two AC16 HP11 guards, spear +3 1d6+1, no multiattack, surrender when one falls; not a mandatory fight or encounter tax. Holding scene has Martha/Jory tokens hidden until visited. No prisoner release automated by UI.

## 07 — Basement encounter: Ilan and the sphere

DM_SECRET. Circular old chamber, stairs south, four broad pillars, knee-high broken masonry, floating perfect dark sphere north center. Arena 30×20 squares at 5 ft/square (map 1536×1024, grid51.2). Stair exit always usable. No VII baked into arena artwork; no future landscape in the map. Reveal Ilan/sphere tokens only on arrival. Sphere public label **Dark sphere**, never Catalyst. Use prepared encounter and existing initiative/HP controls; all restrictions adjudicated manually.

Optional private introductory read-aloud, never automatically displayed:

Kneeling Ilan: **“I have done everything you've asked. Every thief. Every liar. Every murderer. Why do they still resist? Please... tell me what I have done wrong.”** Seeing party: **“You came. You have to see it. It's beautiful. Something beyond our world. Beyond the gods themselves. It sees everything. Every mistake we've ever made. And it has chosen me.”** The sphere gives no definitive reply. These are his beliefs, not confirmed narration.

Enemy stats: Ilan AC15 HP90 speed30, STR10 DEX12 CON14 INT16 WIS12 CHA16, saves WIS+3 CHA+5; one action/turn, **Judicial force** two bolts +5 ranged spell 60ft, each 1d6+3 force, normal adjacent-enemy disadvantage. No legendary actions/resistances; no spell slot drain or mass restraint. Bonus/reaction none. Sphere AC13 HP60 speed0, no attacks, no turns except tracked initiative marker if useful; immune poison/psychic damage and conditions, ordinary weapon/spell damage otherwise works. Not resistant to mundane weapons; critical hits normal. It cannot be negotiated into disappearing.

Temporary restrictions (manual, one active at a time): at initiative20 announce clearly one visible writ and its exact effect; begins only after each PC has had a turn to react, expires next initiative20. It affects Ilan as well. Break the relevant wax seal (AC10 HP5, object at south pillar within easy reach) or use action DC12 Investigation/Arcana to cite its defect; either ends current writ. Seals are mundane objects, described automatically, not hidden precision targets. No effect stacks, repeats on same action, or persists outside chamber. No retroactive punishment.

| Writ | Effect | Counterplay announced with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Haste prohibited | First time each turn a creature moves more than 15 ft, DC12 STR save; fail speed becomes0 for rest of that turn, no damage | Move <=15 ft; use ranged attack; break seal |
| One blow only | A creature's second damaging attack in a turn suffers -2 to attack roll (spells with saves unaffected) | One attack, help, spell, object interaction; cite writ |
| Keep clear | End turn within 10 ft of sphere: DC12 DEX or pushed10 ft away, no damage or opportunity attacks | Attack at reach/range, reposition, destroy seal |

Do not forbid spellcasting, speech, healing, all movement or attacks on sphere. Players can damage it from round1. At first sphere hit, a writ flickers visibly and Ilan struggles: automatic evidence of connection. Arcana DC12 as free observation clarifies disruption, but failure leaves baseline clue. At **30 HP** sphere cracks in apparent depth; current writ ends, all future writs have DC10 and cannot affect more than one creature per round (first violating creature). If Ilan falls first, active writ expires and sphere remains: perceptible pressure persists; attacks can still destroy it. If sphere falls first, all laws cease instantly and Ilan collapses alive/unconscious at1HP if still standing. Healing/nonlethal capture can save him; no mandatory execution. The sphere is actual Catalyst, not Ilan.

Tactics: Ilan stays between party and sphere, uses pillars for half cover, targets conscious threats, never attacks downed PCs; responds to obvious plans, no hidden player knowledge. Can be grappled or disarmed of focus but focus is not sphere vulnerability. Ada/Tomas earned detail causes one lost Ilan action once, not victory by speech. Retreat upstairs ends pursuit; laws do not trap door. After retreat allow recovery; DM records damage/repairs rather than importer resetting HP.

Difficulty estimate, not playtest proof: combined150 HP but only ~13 average bolt damage/round before hit chance; moderate defensive puzzle for four Level4 PCs if sphere can be focused (~3 rounds at20 damage/round). Low resources: Ilan HP65, sphere HP40, one bolt/turn; tell players damage feedback promptly. Strong fresh party: HP90/60 unchanged, use cover well, never add punitive laws midfight. Give safe rest and healing potion from cave or civic first aid before encounter. No automatic character levelling or resource changes.
