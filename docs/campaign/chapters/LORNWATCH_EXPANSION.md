## Glass Road — The Broken Crossing

After the Dealer, the civic report / Mara's duplicate receipt already gives the party a reason to travel toward Lornwatch. Before arrival, the Glass Road crosses an ordinary brook at a storm-damaged timber bridge. Merchant **Elsi Marr**, apprentice **Tavin Holt** and their horse **Bracken** have a wagon caught on the damaged north edge. **Rusk**, a highwayman captain, and three robbers demand the cargo. This is mundane extortion unrelated to any Hollow, sphere, VII, Dealer faction or supernatural conspiracy. Tavin is an adult apprentice. The highwaymen damaged nothing; they opportunistically found the stalled wagon.

Native sequence: existing Veyrholt Final Table → Glass Road crossing → Sunpetal Square, with return links and an alternative south-bank ford. Never require a combat victory/check to continue. Install only additive links to an unambiguous exact existing Final Table; do not copy earlier chapters into the local fixture. Local fixture explicitly starts at the crossing and has no invented earlier-chapter replacement. The DM chooses when to use the travel link after the Dealer outcome.

Arrival: visible demand for cargo, leaning wagon, broken north railing, frightened but uninjured horse, adult apprentice holding a slipping load. Captain and two robbers visible on east bank; one lookout initially hidden west. No initiative until violence, failed threat after a clear warning, or a dangerous rush. Talks/rescue can happen before combat.

Run in 15–25 minutes. Options:

- Fight: captain AC14 HP27; two melee attacks +4, 1d6+2 piercing each OR one crossbow +4, 1d8+2 piercing, 80/320ft. Three highwaymen AC12 HP11; one attack +3, 1d6+1 slashing OR crossbow +3, 1d8+1 piercing, 80/320ft. Speed30. Captain prioritizes cargo, uses cover, never kills downed PCs. Robbers surrender/flee when captain falls or two robbers are disabled. No pursuit across the ford or reinforcements. Four Level4 PCs: short, modest-resource encounter, rescue pressure rather than an attrition boss. Easier: remove lookout and captain makes one melee attack. Harder only if desired: captain HP35, no added attackers.
- Negotiate: captain wants 15gp or two unmarked dry-goods crates, not hostages. An acceptable bargain succeeds without a roll; DC12 Persuasion reduces demand to5gp or a day's work, DC13 Intimidation with credible advantage prompts retreat, DC12 Deception buys two rescue actions. Failure returns to bargaining or withdrawal; no automatic attack. Returning stolen goods/recompense is the party's choice, never an automatic gold change.
- Rescue/repair: secure wagon with rope or strong brace, unload top-heavy crates, then lever wheel onto sound planks. Two distinct sensible actions stabilize it; a sound plan with tools and teamwork succeeds without rolls. Optional DC12 Athletics or carpenter/land-vehicle tools does one step quickly; DC11 Animal Handling calms Bracken, automatically with a slow approach and food. Appropriate spells/features work according to their actual limits. Tavin can be helped onto sound decking automatically from adjacent ground or with a rope. Do not require spell slots.
- Hazard: wagon only deteriorates if combat starts or unsafe heavy movement occurs on the broken strip. Announce creaking and drifting cargo; at end of round2 the loose load begins sliding, at end of round3 one unsecured crate falls into the shallow brook. A secured wagon stops the clock. A creature beside the load can use a reaction DC11DEX to catch the rope; failure loses cargo, not a life. Broken5ft strip is difficult terrain; DC10DEX only if rushing it, failure prone on the deck, no unavoidable lethal fall. Brook is3ft deep, difficult terrain, safe bank exit. South stepping-stone ford costs ten minutes and bypasses the bridge without a roll.
- Creative/bypass: stealth along banks DC12 grants safe starting positions; failure reveals the PCs but no surprise punishment. A decoy cargo, illusion, befriending horse, using ford, escorting travelers on foot or simply declining involvement remain valid. Make results visible, keep all exits usable.

Reward: Elsi offers25gp total and provisions for saving people or recovering the wagon by any route; if cargo lost,10gp and gratitude, no debt for a good-faith attempt. Highwaymen carry8gp total, rope and mundane tools, with no secret letters/symbols. Elsi can accompany party to Lornwatch and point out the Golden Finch/general store, knowing only ordinary road directions. She knows no Hollow facts. If party bypasses, survivors reach town late with ordinary losses; never attach the robbery to Ilan. Report hook survives loss of wagon/report through Mara's established receipt.

DM roles: Elsi is practical, protective of apprentice, values lives over cargo; hides embarrassment about ignoring a bridge maintenance notice, knows load/tools/ford. Tavin is eager, rattled, honest, wants to be useful; knows where spare rope/planks are, not local conspiracies. Rusk is blustering, opportunistic and risk-averse; conceals that his group dislikes a real fight, can accept a face-saving retreat. Bracken is frightened by creaking wood, calms when led to stable ground. All potential reactions are DM reference, never scripted conversations.

Interactive objects: player-readable wagon waybill with mundane supplies/tools/destination; damaged bridge maintenance notice identifying the south ford. Images contain physical evidence only. Wagon is painted into map; its discoverable opens the waybill. DM tracks stability/crates manually. No Catalyst encounter here. After any outcome, use east road or ford to enter Sunpetal Square; no automatic scene activation or party movement.
