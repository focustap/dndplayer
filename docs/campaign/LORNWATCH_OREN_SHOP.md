# Lornwatch — Bramble & Brass: Oren's Weapon Stock

This is a supplemental inventory for **Oren Bramble** at **Bramble & Brass**, the practical general store in Lornwatch. Oren remains a mundane merchant, not an enchanted-weapon dealer. These are ordinary 5e weapons at standard Player's Handbook prices, suitable for an early-level party. Prices are in gold pieces (gp) unless specified.

## DM read-aloud
*Oren sets aside the buckle he's repairing and nods toward a locked rack behind the counter.*

> "Got a few weapons, if that's what you're after. Nothing fancy, nothing enchanted. But they're balanced properly, and I won't sell you something that'll snap the first time you need it."

*He unlocks the rack and spreads a worn cloth across the counter.*

## Shopkeeping notes
- This is a **limited physical inventory**, not an unlimited catalog; deduct purchased quantities.
- Oren does not routinely stock heavy martial weapons, expensive armor, or magical equipment. He can suggest trying a specialist smith in a larger settlement.
- Oren is willing to let a customer inspect a blade or test its balance, but he will not allow reckless swinging in his store.
- The party may ask for a discount; a reasonable roleplayed offer could earn 10% off on a purchase (DM discretion), not automatic free equipment.
- Suggested line if asked for magic: "Magic? No. I sell things that work even when the magic doesn't."
- Keep his existing ordinary goods and the 40 gp livestock/ owlbear work offer unchanged. The weapon stock does not imply Oren knows anything about Lornwatch's hidden supernatural mystery.

## Example dialogue
**If asked what he'd recommend:** "Depends what you know how to use. A good spear'll serve a traveler better than a fancy sword they can't handle."

**If someone asks for the most expensive blade:** "Rapier's twenty-five gold. Nice piece of work, but don't pay extra just to look important."

**If the party asks for enchanted weapons:** "Wrong shop, I'm afraid. I've got sharp steel and honest prices. That's what I can promise."
